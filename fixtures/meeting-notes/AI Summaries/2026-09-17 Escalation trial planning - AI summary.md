## Discussion summary

### Distinguishing acknowledgement from destination

The discussion followed the ownership gap illustrated by the de-identified
ESC-17 example. A case reaching a queue is not the same as a person accepting
the handoff. Participants focused on making that distinction observable rather
than assuming a new destination would, by itself, solve the problem.

Acknowledgement in this trial concerns acceptance of the handoff. It is not
evidence that the technical issue has been resolved or a promise of a resolution
time. Recording a receiving person should help the group examine who accepted
the next action without obscuring work that still needs to happen.
